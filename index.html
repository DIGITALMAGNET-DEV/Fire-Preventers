Introduction

AB&M FIRE PREVENTORS

“AB&M FIRE PREVENTORS” is committed to provide the best possible services to minimize and keep fire risks / hazards “as low as practically possible” to avoid chances of having any fire or life-threatening incidents and thus leading to a loss prevention program.

“AB&M FIRE PREVENTORS” due its exposure, abroad / local trainings and experience is fully capable to meet industrial requirement. The suggested fire safety solutions are backed up with local and international standards and recommended practices like NFPA, BCP 2016, ISO etc.

GAP analysis of existing system of clients or to develop fire prevention program, SOPs, relevant training in the field of fire safety / fire prevention etc are a part of our sustainable services.

The CEO’s notion

Engr. Muhammad Arif Khan

Founder / key facilitator / CEO

What burns Never Returns.

Fire is the best friend and the worst enemy.

I, Engr Muhammad Arif Khan, BE(Mech) / MS (Env), having 35 years of RFF field experience is the founder & key facilitator. Besides local trainings I, have done fire officer, emergency management, command & control courses etc from Singapore and UK. My exposure to fire services Singapore, UK, Canada and USA during trainings and for contract negotiation, procurement and Factory Acceptance of fire trucks for Pakistan Civil Aviation Authority is an added qualification and experience. Factory acceptance testing, site inspections and commissioning of fire safety equipment make me an active, dexterous and pivot in emergency service operations. Working experience at the airports, fuel farms, cargo areas, as trainer at PCAA training center, preparing specs of firefighting support and training equipment is un-matched experience. Based upon international exposure many new concepts / drills and firefighting support equipment has been introduced and implemented to PCAA fire services. As a team lead / additional Director / General Manager RFFS continued dedication and efforts in terms of Managing, monitoring and maintaining ICAO (International Civil Aviation Organization, Montreal Canada) regulatory requirements (ICAO standards & recommended practices / SARPs) at PCAA airports helped PCAA to qualify ICAO audits.

Out of many the above two sayings pushed to form “AB&M FIRE PREVENTERS” with a view to make diligent efforts to minimize fire hazards and help to make our industrial environment and industries fire safe.

After serving 35 years in fire safety on the airports looking after sensitive aircraft operation and carrying out many industrial audits it is felt that at many occasions minor changes in the work theme and a little more value addition, like improved ventilation, housekeeping, appropriate training, suitable fire extinguishers, conducting drills and doing their postmortem etc may lead to appropriate results. However, in many industries having inbuilt fire incidents potential the installation of standpipe system, sprinklers, fire detection system, emergency lighting, means of egress etc are inevitable. Testing of installed systems and conduct of regular drills with improved techniques are vitally important.

Based upon the experience, courses and meetings attended at England, Singapore, USA, Canada, China the industrial requirements are well understood and sustainable solutions are suggested in the light of NFPA, BCP 2016 and best industrial practices. Moreover “AB&M FIRE PREVENTERS” can assess already existing SOPs and / or develop new for the clients. Fire prevention inspection / gap analysis, system testing services etc are on our portfolio.

Volunteer contribution to the nation by the CEO: founder and life member of FPAP (Fire Protection Association of Pakistan) and had been Secretary General of FPAP too. FPAP is a volunteer and non-profit organization serving to enhance awareness and bring modernization to promote fire safety in our society.

From the platform of FPAP, have conducted GAP analysis / fire prevention inspection of major industrial units / hospitals / pharmaceutical companies like Hino Pak, NICVD, Saffron Pharmaceutical (Faisalabad), ICI PAK, Lucky One Mall, Lucky textile, Artistic Milliner etc and walk through inspections of M/s Molty foam, Junaid Jamshed (J.), Feroz 1888, METIER etc.

Besides I was a member of the team to review course curriculum of various Civil Defense Courses.
